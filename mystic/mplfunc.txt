================================================================
 New in 1.11IRC:
   timerms        — Millisecond timer (A3-05)
   formatdate     — Format date with mask string (A3-11)
   datestr fmt 4-6 — 4-digit year (A3-12)
   Record VAR params  — Procedure X(Var R: MyRec) (A1-02)
   Record functions   — Function X : MyRec (A3-04)
   Multi-dim arrays   — Array[1..N, 1..M] in records (A5-02)
   MCI |-Y |-N        — Force Yes/No defaults (A3-06/07)

 See mplref.txt for detailed examples.
 See scripts/testrec.mps and scripts/testarr.mps for samples.
