# Fill Defects (delta)

[◀ Prev: Contents](README.md) · [Contents](README.md) · [Next: FastFont Additions ▶](3.2-fastfont-additions.md)

The three fill defects, their symptoms and their corrections are in the [2.0 techspec](../../2.0/techspecs/2.1-fill-defects.md). This page records what is specific to 3.x - which, for once, is where the evidence actually lives.

- **The 3.0 driver is the source.** `§BUG.6` (pie/chord fill leak) and `§DEAD.7` (patterned flood brush never applied) come from disassembly of **`RIPSCRIP.DLL` v3.0.7** - the driver shipped in RIPtel Visual Telnet 3.1, October 1997 - by the [RIPlib](https://github.com/BradHawthorne/riplib) project (`riplib:docs/spec/11-dll-deviations.md`). Its corrections for both are right, and this repository adopts them; the wider comparison between the two projects' accounts of RIPscrip 3.0 is in [3.0-riplib](../../3.0-riplib/README.md). The attribution therefore runs opposite to the usual delta: the 2.0 page generalizes backward from 3.0 evidence on engine-lineage grounds, while the 3.0 driver is the one client where both defects are directly demonstrated.
- **The leak reaches commands that are new in 3.x.** The generation adds [RIP_SKEWED_OVAL_PIE_SLICE](../ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#rip_skewed_oval_pie_slice) (`|[`), [RIP_SKEWED_OVAL_CHORD](../ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#rip_skewed_oval_chord) (`|+`) and [RIP_FILLED_OVAL_CHORD](../ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#rip_filled_oval_chord) (`|_`) - none of which exists in 2.x - and all three are built on the same arc-plus-radii construction as [RIP_PIE_SLICE](../ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_pie_slice), so they inherit `§BUG.6` by construction. A rotated ellipse's arc endpoints are, if anything, likelier to leave a rasterization gap than an axis-aligned one's. This is the substantive 3.x delta: the defect is 2.x-era, but its blast radius grew when the generation added primitives on top of the defective code.
- **No shipping client is clean.** RIPtel 3.1 is the last TeleGrafix release, so there is no upgrade path to a correct pie fill within the original product line.
- **RIP_FILL is gone from the language but not from the driver.** `§DEAD.7` describes a live flood-fill command in the DLL, border-color branch and all, even though [RIP_FILL](../ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) is absent from the `RIPSCRIP.HLP` inventory and from the corpus. A legacy `!|F` reaching RIPtel 3.1 fills - solid, ignoring the pattern - rather than being discarded. **This corrects a claim previously made in this repository** from the help/corpus silence, that the 3.0 driver dropped the command entirely; positive evidence from the binary supersedes an absent name in the inventory.
- **The corpus proves nothing either way.** The 116 shipping demo scripts render correctly on the driver they were authored against, which is the ordinary outcome - TeleGrafix's own artists worked around whatever misbehaved. Absence of leaking in the corpus is evidence about the artwork, not about the code.
